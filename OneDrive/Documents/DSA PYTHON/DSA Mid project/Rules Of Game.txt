Klondike Solitaire is a popular single-player card game with the following rules and mechanics:

Objective
The main goal of Klondike Solitaire is to move all the cards to four foundation piles, organized by suit (hearts, diamonds, clubs, and spades) in ascending order from Ace to King.

Setup
Deck: A standard deck of 52 playing cards is used.
Tableau: The game starts by dealing cards into seven tableau piles:
The first pile has one card, the second pile has two cards, the third has three cards, and so on, up to the seventh pile, which has seven cards.
Only the top card of each pile is face-up; the rest are face-down.
Stockpile: The remaining cards form the stockpile, which can be drawn from during the game.
Foundation Piles: There are four foundation piles, one for each suit, which start empty.
Gameplay
Moving Cards:

You can move cards between tableau piles according to these rules:
A card can be placed on top of another card if it is of the opposite color (red on black or black on red) and one rank lower (e.g., a red 5 can be placed on a black 6).
An empty tableau pile can only be filled with a King or a sequence starting with a King.
You can move a sequence of cards (a stack) as long as the bottom card is valid for the destination pile.
Foundation Building:

Cards can be moved from the tableau to the foundation piles following these rules:
A foundation pile starts with an Ace and is built up by placing cards in ascending order by rank (2, 3, 4, ..., up to King) and matching the suit.
Drawing Cards:

Players can draw cards from the stockpile (one card at a time or three cards, depending on the rules you set) to assist with moves.
The drawn cards are placed in a waste pile, where the top card can be played.
Flipping Cards:

When you move a card from the tableau, the card beneath it (if any) is flipped face-up.
Winning the Game:

The game is won when all cards are successfully moved to the foundation piles, organized by suit from Ace to King.
Additional Features
Undo/Redo Moves: Some versions of Klondike allow players to undo their last move or redo it if needed.
Hints: Many digital versions provide hints to show possible legal moves.
Strategy Tips
Always try to keep your tableau piles balanced by ensuring that they have a similar number of cards.
Prioritize moving cards to the foundation whenever possible to free up tableau space.
Plan your moves carefully, especially when drawing cards from the stockpile, to maximize your options.
